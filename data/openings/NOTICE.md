Upstream copyright statement: As a collection of facts, this data set is in the public domain. Considerable effort was spent curating and cleaning the data. Insofar as that qualifies for copyright, the work is released under the CC0 Public Domain Dedication.

The five TSV source files are unmodified. `sources.json` records original URLs and SHA-256 digests. `index.json` is generated by `node scripts/setup-openings.mjs` with chess.js: named positions are indexed by EPD (excluding move counters), and each position+move transition in the source lines is indexed as book.

This is a finite opening-name collection, not an exhaustive theory or master-game database. A book label means a recorded opening transition, not an endorsement of move quality. Opening recognition uses positions to support transpositions; the most recent named position is retained after leaving book. Arbitrary setup games are not annotated. Evaluation-based accuracy remains unchanged.
